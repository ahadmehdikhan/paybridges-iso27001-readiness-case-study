# Information Security Policy

**Document Owner:** CISO
**Approved By:** Information Security Steering Committee
**Classification:** Internal
**ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Reference:** Clause 5.2, Control A.5.1
**Review Cycle:** Annual, or upon material change to the business or threat landscape

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes PayBridges' commitment to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets — particularly employee PII, banking data, and payroll records entrusted to us by our customers — and defines the framework under which all subordinate security policies, standards, and procedures operate.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all PayBridges employees, contractors, and third parties with access to PayBridges systems, data, or facilities, and to all information assets within the ISMS boundary defined in `01_ISMS_Boundary_&_Scope.md`.

## 3. Policy Statements

1. PayBridges shall establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve an Information Security Management System (ISMS) conformant to ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
2. Information security objectives shall be defined, measurable, communicated, and reviewed at least annually by the Information Security Steering Committee.
3. The CISO is accountable for the ISMS and reports security posture, risk, and incident metrics to executive leadership and the board on a quarterly cadence.
4. All employees and contractors are responsible for protecting information assets in accordance with subordinate policies (Access Control, Classification, Change Management, Asset Management) and role-specific security training.
5. Security requirements shall be considered at every stage of the product and engineering lifecycle ("secure by design"), consistent with Control A.5.8 and A.8.25–A.8.29.
6. Non-conformance with this policy or its subordinate policies is addressed through the Disciplinary Process (Control A.6.4) and may result in termination of access, employment, or contract.
7. Legal, regulatory, and contractual information security obligations (GDPR, UK DPA, US state privacy law, customer DPAs) shall be identified, tracked, and satisfied — see Control A.5.31.
8. Exceptions to this policy require documented risk acceptance signed by the CISO and, where risk exceeds defined risk appetite thresholds, by the Steering Committee.

## 4. Governance Structure

| Body | Responsibility | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Information Security Steering Committee | Policy approval, risk acceptance above appetite threshold, ISMS performance review | Quarterly |
| CISO | Day-to-day ISMS ownership, incident escalation, control operation oversight | Continuous |
| Internal Audit / Risk Advisory (this engagement) | Independent testing of control design and operating effectiveness | Annual, pre-certification |

## 6. Policy Enforcement

Violations are reported through the Information Security Event Reporting channel (Control A.6.8) and investigated per the Incident Management process (Controls A.5.24–A.5.28). Confirmed violations are escalated to People/HR for disciplinary action.
